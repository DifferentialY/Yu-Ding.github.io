Yu Ding

Assistant Professor
Okayama Prefectural University

I am an Assistant Professor in the Faculty of Computer Science and Systems Engineering at Okayama Prefectural University. I received my Ph.D. in Mathematics and Informatics from the University of Toyama in 2026.

Previously, I received my M.S. in Software Engineering from Harbin University of Science and Technology in 2021 and my B.S. in Animal Science and Technology from Northeast Agricultural University in 2018.

My research interests include deep learning, representation learning, and multi-view learning.
